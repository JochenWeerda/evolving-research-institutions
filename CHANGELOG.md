# Changelog

All notable changes to *Evolving Research Institutions* are documented here.

## v0.1 — 2026-08-28

Initial public working-paper release.

- Defines durable AI research institutions built from large populations of
  ephemeral agents.
- Treats population size—ultimately millions of parallel instances—as a key
  experimental variable rather than assuming that model parameter count alone
  determines discovery rate.
- Makes independently reproduced knowledge, not raw activity or duplicated
  output, the basis for allocating future compute.
- Proposes anti-duplication infrastructure, verifier institutions, controlled
  consolidation, safety gates, falsifiable hypotheses, and a staged scaling
  ladder.
- Publishes citation and Zenodo metadata under CC BY 4.0.
