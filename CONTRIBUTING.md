# Contributing to ERI

ERI is an open research proposal. Contributions are welcome when they make the architecture more testable, reproducible, secure, or clearly distinguished from prior work.

## High-value contributions

- prior art with precise citations and a short relevance note;
- formal specifications for claims, provenance, institutions, or treasury events;
- benchmark generators with hidden ground truth;
- baseline implementations for the six experimental arms;
- mechanisms for novelty measurement and productive deduplication;
- replication, falsification, anti-collusion, and Sybil-resistance tests;
- economic and evolutionary simulations;
- safety threat models and measurable deployment gates;
- negative results that challenge the central hypothesis.

## Research standards

1. Separate observations, hypotheses, and empirical results.
2. Include enough information to reproduce quantitative claims.
3. Report compute, model, prompt, tool, and sampling conditions.
4. Preserve negative results and failed replications.
5. Do not use private data, unauthorized systems, real-world sabotage, credential attacks, or uncontrolled dual-use experiments.
6. Treat agent "survival" as resource allocation, not a claim about sentience.

## Licensing

By contributing prose or diagrams, you agree to license the contribution under CC BY 4.0. Code contributions should be placed under Apache-2.0 with an SPDX identifier unless a maintainer explicitly documents another compatible license.
